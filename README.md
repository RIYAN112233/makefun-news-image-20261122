# The World on One Page — Sunday 22 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122):
sum up the world news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in one image.

The image is a newspaper front page: a masthead naming the day, and six
panels, one per story, each a flat woodcut-style editorial illustration
with a headline and a short factual caption. The panels were drawn as
one six-panel sheet, sliced apart, and the page was then assembled and
typeset programmatically, so nothing in the page is model-drawn text.
`main.ts`
(Node 22, standard library only) copies the finished `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`; it runs offline and writes identical bytes on
every run.

## The six stories, and where they were reported

1. **Middle East — the Strait of Hormuz.** Bahrain said an Iranian
   strike set a civilian industrial site ablaze; Tehran said it hit US
   bases. Two oil tankers were struck near the Strait of Hormuz, and
   Saudi-led forces intercepted three Houthi missiles aimed at Riyadh.
   (World in 90 Seconds briefing, 22 Nov 2026; News Media House,
   22 Nov 2026.)
2. **Climate — Antalya, Türkiye.** The COP31 climate summit entered a
   second day of overtime, split over fossil-fuel language and money
   for poorer nations; the EU said it wanted a good deal or no deal.
   (One Turn daily digest, Turkey, 22 Nov 2026; Daily Perspective,
   22 Nov 2026.)
3. **Science — mathematics.** Mathematician Levent Alpöge says
   Anthropic's Claude helped him prove the Riemann hypothesis,
   unsolved for over 160 years; two groups have verified the claim.
   (One Turn daily digest, Science, 22 Nov 2026, reporting the
   Guardian, BBC News and DW.)
4. **Africa — Nigeria.** A boat carrying mourners to a funeral
   overturned in southern Nigeria; at least 24 people died and 24
   were rescued. (One Turn daily digest, Disaster, 22 Nov 2026;
   News Media House, 22 Nov 2026.)
5. **Health — Manipur, India.** At least 11 people died and more than
   180 were taken to hospital after drinking fake liquor in Manipur;
   symptoms pointed to methanol poisoning. (One Turn daily digest,
   India, 22 Nov 2026; World in 90 Seconds briefing, 22 Nov 2026.)
6. **Economy — EU and Indonesia.** The European Union and Indonesia
   signed a trade agreement after a decade of talks, removing almost
   all tariffs between them; the deal covers 730 million people.
   (One Turn daily digest, Indonesia, 22 Nov 2026, reporting
   France 24, DW and Euronews.)
